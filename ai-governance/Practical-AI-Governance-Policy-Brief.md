**POLICY BRIEF SERIES · EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW**
Series: Series 2026-01 · Practical AI governance framework
Prepared by: Michael Paulauski
Date: November 15, 2026
Subject: Executive overview and talking points across six federal AI pillars

### Bottom line up front

Polling from the Pew Research Center and community-backlash research from the Brookings Institution show why voters across regions are demanding stronger AI rules: rising electric bills, dry-basin water strain, unpermitted gas turbines near neighborhoods, workplace surveillance, algorithmic rent and insurance denials, and anxiety over career displacement (with 71% expecting fewer total U.S. jobs, led by 73% among adults under 30). Families and clinicians are also sounding alarms over manipulative youth AI companions and lab safety corner-cutting. At the same time, Stanford AI Index, CSIS, and Lowy Institute tracking confirms that unilateral U.S. software freezes fail across borders: top U.S.-China frontier gaps narrowed to 2.7% on LMArena while DeepSeek and Alibaba open-weight models sit within 3.3% of closed leaders.

The governing principle is to separate open research speed from accountable deployment speed: let American researchers, universities, and startups build without Big Tech bottlenecks while enforcing binding public-interest rules wherever commercial or government AI touches power, water, and air, civil rights, courts, health care, paychecks, deepfakes and creative work, catastrophic bio-cyber risks, and minors.

### 1. Make hyperscale data centers fund clean power and water up front

Families never asked to bankroll Big Tech server farms through higher utility bills, depleted aquifers, or unpermitted neighborhood gas plants. Every facility or campus over 20 megawatts (aggregated across phased and BTM sites) should pay its full infrastructure and pollution tab up front.

* Enforce 100% cost-causation grid tariffs (including dedicated supply) with minimum-term collateral across FERC, ERCOT/non-RTO regulators, and state utility commissions so data-center debt never hits household bills.
* Reserve fast-track queues for projects bringing deliverable clean power or bridge contracts on a rising hourly schedule plus 0.25% to 1% annual curtailment across 85-366 peak hours (76-126 GW system headroom via shifting/clean storage instead of fossil backup), reconductoring, and competitive replacement at fossil sites.
* Condition federal water grants, DOE loans/cloud deals, and state drought/groundwater caps on ending bulk potable discounts and using storage-buffered closed-loop, zero-drought hybrid adiabatic, or reclaimed water, and close Subpart KKKKa (24-mo, <=45% CF, <=850 MMBtu/h), RIN 2060-AX08, CAA §§ 110(f)/112(i)(4), and 202(c) retirement/50-hr backup waivers.

Full issue brief (Brief 2026-01A): [Clean power, water, and air rules for hyperscale data centers](./power-water/power-water-brief.md)

### 2. Break Big Tech compute bottlenecks and ban algorithmic price-fixing

Four or five hyperscale cloud conglomerates should not control who gets to train AI or use shared pricing engines to squeeze renters and shoppers.

* Expand NSF's Sept. 2026 NAIRR Operations Center via the CREATE AI Act with the full $2.6B six-year appropriations target for national-lab/university supercomputers and zero-egress cloud vouchers instead of revocable Big Tech credits.
* Protect open-weight AI distribution below Pillar 6's narrow catastrophic bio-cyber tripwires (Brief 2026-01F) from licensing bans, paired with NSTM-6 DNA screening and cyber-hardening, instead of forcing civic institutions onto closed corporate APIs.
* Block dominant cloud-compute tying and chip bottlenecks, mandate HSR review on non-exclusive/equity acqui-hires, and ban algorithms pooling/training weights on nonpublic data or facilitating price coordination while sparing tenant-isolated internal/public-data tools.

Full issue brief (Brief 2026-01B): [Public compute, open-weight models, and algorithmic antitrust](./compute-antitrust/compute-antitrust-brief.md)

### 3. Set statutory civil-liberties, courtroom, and patient-care guardrails while expanding indigent defense and vetted legal-aid tools

Constitutional limits cannot depend on private vendor contracts, and technology should expand human access to justice without automating surveillance, sentencing, or insurance denials.

* Enact permanent statutory and defense prohibitions requiring human command over lethal or dual-use strikes (preserving incoming/UAS point defense), ban autonomous nuclear warning/launch, require warrants (with 48-hr exigencies) for 1:N biometric/camera dragnets, and close the broker loophole.
* Expand legal-aid/public-defender hiring alongside confidentiality-protected civil tools and Byrne JAG / Defender discovery AI, require explainable human findings in federal sentencing/benefits, exempt defender grants from Byrne JAG unvalidated-risk-score rules, and finalize Oct. 2026 Rule 707/901(c) reforms.
* Permanently halt WISeR and ban automated coverage delays or denials across Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, FEHB, ERISA, and commercial plans without relevant-specialty clinical review (allowing fast-track approvals), ban denial quotas, and enact explicit ERISA §§ 502(a)/514(b)(2)(B), MA, and FEHBA non-preemption carve-outs.

Full issue brief (Brief 2026-01C): [Statutory civil-liberties, courtroom, and patient-care guardrails](./civil-liberties/civil-liberties-brief.md)

### 4. Narrow the worker-capital tax gap, ban abusive "bossware," and insure displaced workers

Tax law should stop paying corporations a bonus to replace human staff solely for tax arbitrage, and workers need concrete income security and bargaining power when capable AI agents transform white-collar roles.

* Taper corporate/pass-through 100% bonus depreciation toward MACRS while preserving Section 174A R&D, matched on-prem inference recovery, Section 179, grid, 35% Section 48D chips, and restored clean-energy credits, funding with base broadeners a FICA-indexed credit keeping Trust Funds whole.
* Require documented human override authority across employer and vendor/1099 intermediaries, ban injury quotas ("bossware") and AI-claim forced arbitration, add advance notice/civil penalties, and mandate NLRA pre-deployment bargaining over surveillance and quotas plus effects bargaining over displacement.
* Replace broken vouchers with 50% wage-loss insurance ($15,000-$20,000/yr cap, 1+ yr tenure), 180-day rolling WARN notice/severance with FUTA § 3304(a) UI non-offset rules, MDRC-backed apprenticeships, and W-2/MEWA-solvent ERISA portable benefit pools.

Full issue brief (Brief 2026-01D): [Worker tax parity, workplace AI rules, and displacement insurance](./workers-taxes/workers-taxes-brief.md)

### 5. Close civil deepfake gaps, stop voice-clone fraud, and protect creator rights

Voters and working artists deserve real recourse against sexualized deepfakes, senior voice-clone scams, and uncompensated piracy without censoring parody or tracking everyday users.

* Amend Pub. L. 119-12 (TAKE IT DOWN) with bad-faith notice penalties without NCII put-backs, pass DEFIANCE (S. 1837 knowing/reckless + profiteer suits), and pass a revised NO FAKES banning lifetime assignments and clickwrap licenses without staydown filters or 512(h) subpoenas.
* Enforce and finalize FTC Part 461 (individuals), FCC 24-17/24-84, and FCC 25-25 non-IP caller-ID rules, mandate RFC 3161-timestamped hosted C2PA/watermark provenance (zero user IDs, never open weights/text), and bar state penalties on unsigned citizen or open-source media.
* Mandate high-level training summaries, penalize shadow libraries and paywall/Section 1201 research-breach laundering (sparing open URL indexes), protect non-market-diluting fair use, and add antitrust-safe voluntary licensing with direct creator payout splits.

Full issue brief (Brief 2026-01E): [Intimate-deepfake remedies, voice-clone fraud, and creator rights](./deepfakes-creators/deepfakes-creators-brief.md)

### 6. Govern deployment pace, frontier bio-cyber risks, and youth chatbots without surveillance IDs

Answering real fears about AI safety, children, and unchecked speed means putting strict design duties and physical choke points in law while refusing intrusive government-ID checkpoints.

* Protect open R&D speed while enforcing sector-specific pre-deployment safety standards in high-stakes domains, paired with NIST/DOE export-controlled cluster compute-verification research (excluding consumer GPUs).
* Pass the AI Whistleblower Protection Act (S. 1792), authorize non-regulatory NIST CAISI, set a floor-only federal SB 53 / RAISE baseline ($500M / 10^26 FLOPs, 72-hour critical and 24-hour imminent incident notices, preserving stronger state remedies), require capability-gated bio-cyber red-teaming (banning general compute licensing), and enact expanded statutory NSTM-6 encrypted gene-synthesis screening.
* Outlaw deceptive human/therapist impersonation, minor-directed sexualized or romantic roleplay, self-harm reinforcement, and retention loops on commercial companion bots and companion modes (with private suits), while banning KYC IDs on general AI and requiring blind-signed, zero-attestation cryptographic age tokens where adult checks apply.

Full issue brief (Brief 2026-01F): [Frontier safety, development pace, and youth protections without ID dragnets](./safety-youth-pace/safety-youth-pace-brief.md)

### Trade-offs to weigh in policy design

**01 · Permitting Scope**
Fast-tracking clean energy infrastructure can fracture center-left coalitions if bills sweep too broadly. Statutory fast-tracks must stay strictly scoped to interregional transmission timelines, reconductoring, dynamic line ratings, geothermal exploration, and battery/zero-carbon projects on low-conflict or remediated sites, preserving Tribal consultation and cumulative fenceline safeguards everywhere and never granting fast-tracks or weaker NEPA and Clean Air Act enforcement to fossil projects, as demanded by the Sierra Club and frontline community groups.

**02 · Tax Parity vs. Robot Taxes**
Tax economists across the spectrum warn that literal 'robot taxes' or compute levies are nearly impossible to define and risk driving investment abroad, while MDRC trials (Per Scholas / St. Nicks) caution that sectoral training needs deep employer buy-in to scale. Tapering bonus depreciation plus base broadeners supports FICA-indexed credits and wage insurance without taxing compute.

**03 · Public Compute, Open Weights & Frontier Tripwires**
Appropriated NAIRR compute and licensing-free open weights check oligopoly capture. Pre-release red-teaming applies only above efficiency-indexed catastrophic bio/cyber tripwires with an explicit open-weight harbor below those limits (never shielding CSAM, shadow-library piracy, or intimate deepfakes), paired with physical NSTM-6 DNA screening.

**04 · Courtroom Access vs. Hallucinations**
Low-income litigants rarely have a second lawyer to catch AI hallucinations that Stanford studies still document in legal tools. Publicly funded legal-aid software requires independent accuracy audits, court-compatible, attorney-vetted pro se templates, and human attorney sign-off on open-ended generative briefs.

**05 · Likeness Rights & Creator Pay vs. Free Expression**
Overbroad likeness or watermark laws can chill satire, reporting, and open tools. Limiting synthetic provenance to hosted commercial generators without user IDs, barring state penalties on unsigned open media, protecting parody/news/fair use, and targeting shadow-library piracy protects victims and creators cleanly.

**06 · Youth Companion Safety vs. KYC IDs**
Bills like the GUARD Act risk creating centralized ID/biometric dragnets post-Paxton that break on open operating systems and invite NetChoice injunctions where age gates burden non-erotic speech. Regulating manipulative companion product design directly and requiring zero-retention, non-linkable age tokens protects both kids and user privacy.
